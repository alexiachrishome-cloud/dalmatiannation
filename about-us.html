About us

Who we are and what we're building.

We are two Dalmatian parents living in Bristol, UK, who are a little jaded by the difficulties in finding good dog food for our pooches.

Why we started

Two dogs, one recipe change, and a lot of cleaning up.

This isn't a brand that decided Dalmatians were a nice niche. It's two people who got let down by the food they trusted and decided to fix it themselves.

01

The recipe that changed overnight

Our girls were on a big-name, "Dalmatian-friendly" food. Then the recipe quietly changed — no announcement, just two poorly dogs and a forum full of owners saying exactly the same thing.

02

Purines are a Dalmatian thing

Dalmatians can have a gene that changes how they process purines, which can make them prone to urate crystals and stones — especially on a high-purine diet. A DNA test can tell you about your own dog, but a purine-controlled diet from the start is a simple way to help reduce the risk.

So, after some rather gross (oooh - yes, both ends) experiences, we've flipped the narrative - a brand for Dalmatians (and pooches with urinary issues) which is enjoyable for all; rather than a generic food that owners have to spend nights trawling the ingredients of.

What we're building

Food first. Then everything that actually helped us.

  • A purine-controlled complete food designed around Dalmatians and other pooches that have urinary issues (not to cure, but to help manage), made in the UK and tested on our very own pooches!
  • Treats that follow the same rules — because "Dalmatian-safe" shouldn't stop at the main meal.
  • Kit we actually use: ultrasonic whistles, vibration collars for deaf dogs, and beds that survive.
  • First-hand advice on training, sibling squabbles and living with a dog who can't hear you.

We're not vets — but we're not doing this alone. Our recipes are developed with a qualified animal feed nutritionist and industry professionals. Always check with your own vet, especially if your dog has a diagnosed condition. What we will do is tell you exactly what's in the bowl — and email you before anything ever changes.
